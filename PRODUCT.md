# Product

## Register

product

(Primary surfaces are interactive app UI — live chat, garden, profiles, friend
feed, admin. The homepage is a brand/welcome surface and is treated as brand
when worked on directly.)

## Users
Members of a small, friendly Discord gaming community ("OURCHAT Teaparty").
They drop in between play sessions to chat live, tend a shared garden that
grows with activity, decorate a profile card, find people to play with, and
collect badges. Context: casual, social, often late-night, on both desktop and
phone. They're here to feel welcomed and to hang out, not to get a job done.

## Product Purpose
A cozy home base for the community that's warmer and more personal than Discord
alone: real-time presence, a living garden tied to participation, customizable
member cards, and lightweight games. Success = the place feels alive, inviting,
and worth returning to daily.

## Brand Personality
Cozy, warm, gentle, playful, handcrafted. Three words: **warm, soft, whimsical**.
A teaparty in a sunlit cottage garden. Voice is friendly and unhurried ("pull
up a chair", "welcome home"). Delight via hand-drawn glyphs, steam, drifting
clouds, growing plants — never slick or corporate.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards (sharp corners, gray-on-white, dense data).
- Corporate/fintech minimalism; cold neutrals; aggressive gradients.
- Discord's own dark-blurple UI (this is the warm alternative to it).
- "AI landing page" tropes: tiny tracked uppercase eyebrows on every section,
  01/02/03 numbered markers, gradient text, identical icon-card grids.

## Design Principles
1. **Cozy first.** Every surface should feel warm, soft, and inviting before
   it's efficient. Rounded, layered, gently lit.
2. **Alive, not static.** Presence, growth, and motion show the place is
   breathing — steam rises, plants grow, friends appear online.
3. **Handcrafted delight.** Custom glyphs and illustration over stock/emoji;
   small surprises reward attention.
4. **Calm legibility.** Playfulness never costs readability or contrast; the
   warmth is in color and shape, text stays clear.
5. **Preserve the identity.** Elevate the existing pastel-teaparty system;
   don't reinvent the brand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Target WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1, including on tinted/
  pastel surfaces and placeholders.
- Light + dark themes both first-class.
- Every animation needs a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative.
- Keyboard-navigable controls; visible focus states.
